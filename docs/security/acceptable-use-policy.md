# Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective date:** 9 May 2026

## Summary

By using the BHN Training Platform you agree to these rules. They keep the platform safe, useful, and respectful for everyone.

## Use the platform for what it's for

- Take and create training content; apply for and post internships; communicate professionally with the people you're matched with.
- Don't probe the platform's security posture without going through our [security disclosure policy](https://bhn-training-platform.vercel.app/security).

## Account responsibility

- You are responsible for activity under your account.
- Don't share your password. Enable multi-factor authentication.
- Tell us at `security@biohubnetwork.ca` if you suspect your account is compromised.

## Content rules

You agree NOT to upload, post, or transmit:
- Sexually explicit material; threats, harassment, hate speech
- Personal data of someone other than yourself (or someone who has authorised you)
- Patient health records (PHI). BHN is not configured for PHI processing
- Malware, phishing payloads, or malicious links
- Content you don't hold the rights to share
- Content that could mislead readers about credentials, training records, or hiring outcomes

## What we may do

- Suspend or close accounts that violate these rules
- Remove content; preserve copies for compliance / law-enforcement responses
- Cooperate with law-enforcement requests that are legally compelled
- Make best efforts to keep service available, but no platform is 100% uptime

## Acceptable automated use

- Don't scrape the platform faster than a human would browse it
- Don't make automated requests using credentials that aren't yours
- Don't use BHN to train AI models without explicit written agreement

## Enterprise / employer accounts

- If you are using BHN as part of a contract with your employer, your employer's policies + their DPA with BHN apply alongside this AUP
- Employers must not share applicant data outside their hiring decision process
- Misuse of applicant data is a contract violation, not just an AUP violation

## Reporting

- Vulnerability reports → `security@biohubnetwork.ca` ([disclosure policy](https://bhn-training-platform.vercel.app/security))
- Abuse / Trust & Safety → `safety@biohubnetwork.ca` (TBD — replace when configured)
- Privacy / GDPR / PIPEDA requests → `privacy@biohubnetwork.ca` (TBD)

## Enforcement

We may enforce this AUP without notice in cases of:
- Active security incidents
- Active fraud
- Imminent risk to other users

For lesser violations we'll typically warn first.

## Changes

We'll update this AUP as the platform evolves. Material changes are announced via the in-platform changelog and (for enterprise customers) via your account contact.
