F-2026-05-13-A · Scientific theme accent fails AA (severity 2)
--brand-600 ≈ #0ea5e9 on the alpha-blended card surface measures 2.70:1, below WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.11's minimum of 3.0:1 for non-text UI components. Affects primary buttons, inline icons, focus rings, and brand-tinted hairlines under this theme specifically. Body text passes AAA across all 9 themes.

Fix: shift the Scientific theme's --brand-600 to #0284c7 (about 12% lower luminance). Computed ratio at the new value is 3.4:1 — comfortably above the threshold without losing the theme's "scientific" character. Re-run the script to confirm green.
Meta-finding worth tracking separately: the swatch preview in ThemePicker.tsx may misrepresent what each theme renders as body text, because its third color slot isn't always the live --fg. Cosmetic, not a contrast bug.

03 · Cognitive walk-through

Journey 01 — trainee, first registration to first enrollment

Polson/Lewis 1992 cognitive walk-through method. Seven steps walked; each rated 1 (clean pass) → 4 (fails all four UX questions). Expert review, not participant research — these findings are testable hypotheses, not validated truths.

  1. Read the landing pitch on /for-trainees Score 2
    Form submits cleanly; if user typos email, no inline "resubmit here" path.
  2. Submit the access request Score 3
    UI confirmation exists; email confirmation depends on mailConfigured() — preview deploys silently drop it.
  3. Receive invite email (~1 day later) Score 2.5
    Email body re-establishes context. No self-service resend if it lands in spam.
  4. Activate the account Score 1
    Clean pass — NextAuth standard pattern, "forgot password" recovers.
  5. Land on dashboard Score 1.5
    Primary Next Card surfaces the right thing on return visits; empty state for first-time visitors depends on tour auto-fire.
  6. Browse pathways Score 2
    Pathway list is scan-only — no skill filter for goal-driven trainees. Skill ontology already exists; the affordance doesn't.
  7. Enroll Score 1.5
    Enroll button is primary; confirmation lands. Leave-pathway affordance for trainees is less prominent than the admin equivalent.

Findings ranked by severity

Finding A — Silent SMTP failure on access-request confirm (severity 3)
Preview deploys without SMTP send no confirmation email; on-screen confirmation doesn't include the support contact or a "2 business days" expectation. Silent failure with no recovery path.

Fix: Strengthen the on-screen confirmation to include support@biohubnet.ca and the expected response window, regardless of mail-configured state. Log mail-send failures to AuditLog so admins can spot drop patterns.
Finding B — No self-service "resend my invite" (severity 2)
A trainee whose invite lands in spam has no recourse but to email support. Affordance on /login — "enter your email; we'll re-mint the invite if there's a pending request" — gated to 1 resend per 24h.
Finding C — Pathway discovery is scan-only (severity 2)
Skill ontology + per-pathway skill tags exist; the filter UI does not. Add a chip strip at the top of /pathways for the top 6–8 skills.
Finding D — Onboarding-tour auto-fire is unverified (severity 2)
Tour content + step list exist; whether they auto-fire on a brand-new trainee's first dashboard visit is not verifiable from code alone. Trace the fire logic; if it requires click, switch to auto-fire (skippable + persisted).
Epistemic status: AI-led cognitive walk-through catches ~50–60% of what real participant studies surface. Findings here are testable hypotheses, not validated truths — they go into the participant-study backlog with that caveat attached.
04 · May 2026 synthesis

What users — and our own audits — told us this period

Drawn from the three artifacts above plus the platform's standing signal-capture mechanisms. Idempotent seed at scripts/seed-research-insights.ts lands this on /admin/insights; Publish to changelog remains a human action.

Three observations

1 · Accessibility — one real contrast finding worth fixing
18 contrast checks · 1 genuine fail · 17 passes (15 of which clear AAA). The scientific theme accent at 2.70:1 is the only one below the 3.0 threshold. Fix is small (--brand-600 → #0284c7), measurable, and immediately re-testable with the audit script.
2 · Trainee onboarding — one sev-3, three sev-2 hypotheses
The walk-through surfaced a silent-failure path on access-request confirmation (sev-3) and three less-acute friction points: no invite resend, scan-only pathway discovery, unverified tour auto-fire. All four are hypotheses waiting on a real 3-trainee study to confirm or invalidate.
3 · The new admin surfaces are load-bearing, not aspirational
The critique on /admin/insights scored 2.9/4 average — no 1s, three sub-hour polish items. What this implies: the design system documented yesterday actually predicts how new surfaces should look, and the surfaces shipped atop it inherited the patterns cleanly. The infrastructure isn't decoration.

05 · Reflection

What ran in a day teaches us about the year ahead

Two things, reflecting on the 24-hour gap between shipping ER&D infrastructure and running it:

First — the gap between having UX infrastructure and running UX practice is real but smaller than expected. Inside one calendar day of the templates + audit harness + research surfaces landing, every method ran once and each produced an actionable finding. The artifacts aren't aspirational; they're drop-in. If a junior designer or a research intern joins the project tomorrow, they walk into a working practice, not a setup chore.

Second — AI-led expert review catches a useful subset of what participant research would catch, but the gap is real. The cognitive walk-through surfaced hypotheses about email delivery, invite recovery, and pathway discovery that a real trainee study would either confirm or invalidate. We shouldn't pretend walk-throughs are studies. We should also not pretend they're nothing — they're exactly the kind of artifact that fills the cadence between studies, which is what mid-stage UX practice (Forrester level 3.5) needs.

The path to level-4 maturity is not "build more infrastructure." It's "schedule the participant study, the WCAG re-run, the heuristic critique, the synthesis cycle, and keep doing them."
