Delete button · 50 variations

Saved survey of delete-button design directions. Five groups — minimal micro-animations, geometric / architectural, 8-bit Nintendo, industrial / alarm, and playful. No thick lines anywhere; all rely on motion + colour + glyph for the warning signal. Hover any button to see its behaviour. Click to feel the press / active state. Each is keyboard-focusable.
