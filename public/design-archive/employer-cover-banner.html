HR cover banner — wavy aurora hero (archived 2026-05-21)

The hero from the original HR home page at /employer — a 5-stop deep-violet-to-pink gradient stage with four aurora blurs and a fractal-noise overlay. Used as the full-bleed backdrop behind the company identity row from the spring 2026 design through May 2026, when the HR home was consolidated into the workspace at /employer/postings. Archived here as a reusable reference — the gradient stops, aurora positions, noise opacity, and eyebrow chrome are all preserved.

Brand stage

Anatomy

The banner is six stacked layers. Order matters — bottom up.

1 · Base gradient

linear-gradient(135deg, #0b0f24 0%, #142046 25%, #312e81 50%, #6b21a8 75%, #831843 100%) — 5-stop deep navy → indigo → violet → magenta. Sets the dramatic violet-pink stage.

2 · Four auroras

Cyan top-left, pink bottom-right-of-centre, amber top-right, lime bottom-left-of-centre. Each is a radial gradient at blur(48px) with opacity 0.4–0.7. The colour pairing covers all four corners of the colour wheel so any composited content sits warmly regardless of position.

3 · Fractal noise grain

SVG feTurbulence + feColorMatrix at mix-blend-mode: overlay with opacity 0.22. Softens the gradient banding, lifts the colours toward a printed-poster feel.

4 · Mid-line tick

A 1-pixel-tall horizontal line at 50% Y, dimmed at the edges via a transparent-to-15%-white-to-transparent gradient. Reads as a horizon line; subtle, mostly subconscious.

5 · Bottom shadow

A 10rem tall gradient at the bottom of the stage, transparent at the top, 40% black at the bottom. Lets the identity row above the banner read clearly against the dark fade-out.

6 · Eyebrow chrome

A short white tick + the BRAND STAGE label at top-left, 10px caps with 0.28em tracking. The only UI element on the banner; everything else is decorative.
