Delete button · prism plastic-cover variations

Twelve delete-switch designs exploring plastic covers with angled facets — each cover is a single flat plane on top of a glowing DELETE button, but its surface geometry (chamfers, ridges, conic facets, fresnel rings) creates a different reflection signature. Click any switch to flip the cover open and back closed; the base button glows through while closed. None of these commit anything — they're visual studies for the LaunchSwitch family.

All twelve covers flip with the same animation primitive: transform-origin: top center; rotateX(-85deg) with a cubic-bezier on close (slight overshoot, "click shut") and ease-out on open. The reflection / refraction effects come entirely from stacked CSS gradients (linear, conic, radial) — no images, no SVG, no JS beyond a class toggle. Reflections are static, picking a fixed light direction (top-left, ~135°) so they read as moulded geometry rather than animated highlights. Picking the right one for production means deciding how "industrial" vs. "product" the surface wants to feel: facets and bevels read industrial; refractive / fresnel reads softer and more product-design.
