C · Type-on scaffold

Blank sheet appears instantly. Lines "type themselves" in a rapid typewriter sweep — name, contact, three sections each with body lines. Cursor blinks at the end. Feels fast · technical.

D · Origami unfold · 3D

Real perspective — the sheet starts as a folded quarter; the right half flips open around the centre vertical fold (you see the paper's back face during the rotation), then the bottom half flips down around the centre horizontal fold. Lays flat with a soft scale-overshoot, crease lines linger. Feels crafted · tactile · the paper has mass.
