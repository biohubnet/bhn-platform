D · Origami unfold · 3D (dramatised)

Sheet sits at a persistent ~12° tilt toward the camera, so depth is visible at rest. Right half flips open around the vertical fold over 460ms with a clearly dark BACKFACE visible mid-rotation + a drop shadow growing under the flap. Lays flat with a soft tilt → flat correction, scale-overshoot, crease lines fade in then settle. Total ~1300ms. Feels tactile · the paper has real mass.
