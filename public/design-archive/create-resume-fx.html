C · Type-on scaffold

Blank sheet appears instantly. Lines "type themselves" in a rapid typewriter sweep — name, contact, three sections each with body lines. Cursor blinks at the end. Feels fast · technical.

D · 3D fold-down · single dramatic

Sheet sits at a permanent ~22° tilt toward the camera — even at rest. Only ONE fold runs: the bottom half hinges down from behind the top half over 620ms, with a dark slate-cream backface visible the whole first half of the rotation, a hard drop shadow growing through the 90° point, and a soft overshoot landing. Single clean 3D motion your eye can lock onto. Feels weighty · architectural · a real piece of paper opening.
