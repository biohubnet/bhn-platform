Delete-cover exploration — 20 protective patterns

The current implementation (LaunchSwitch) uses a frosted-glass lid. Honest take: it doesn't read as "protective cover" — the blur softens the glow but the affordance is ambiguous. Below are 20 alternative patterns, organised loosely by metaphor: mechanical / breakable / locks / tear-off / holds / sequence / decorative. Each is a click-to-trigger preview; click Reset on any card to bring the cover back. Pick a favourite and I'll port it into the production LaunchSwitch component.

01 · Brushed steel flip lid

Classic missile-launch cover. Hinged at the top with visible screws. Reads as serious industrial-safety.

02 · Red translucent ABS plastic

Like the classic LaunchSwitch but coloured red plastic instead of frosted glass — clearly a "guard", you can faintly see the button through it.

03 · Hazard-striped flip cover

Yellow/black hazard stripes — instantly recognisable as "do not touch". Maybe TOO loud for a calm app, but works as an unambiguous signal.

04 · Horizontal sliding shutter

Steel shutter slides right to expose the button — like a guillotine garage door. Different gesture from the flip patterns.

05 · Vertical sliding shutter

Slides up (like a sci-fi door). Pairs well with vertical lists of cards.

06 · Vault combination dial

Rotate the dial to unlock. Slowest pattern here — the most deliberate gesture. Pairs with destructive bulk operations.

07 · Break-glass emergency panel

"In case of emergency, break glass". Single click cracks; second click shatters. Theatrical but the metaphor is universally read.

08 · Clear acrylic shield

Crystal-clear acrylic — looks like real museum-quality plastic, not a smoke-haze blur. Fades + scales on activation.

09 · Wax seal

Old-fashioned red wax stamp with monogram. Cracks open with a satisfying rotate-and-fade. Quirky / editorial.

10 · Padlock click-to-unlock

Small padlock — the shackle swings open. Click the body again to confirm. Two clicks, very clear "armed then fire".

11 · Twist-lock cap

Like a child-proof medicine cap. Twist + lift. Friction-rich motion communicates difficulty.

12 · Keyed safety switch

Turn the brass key 90° to arm. Industrial control-panel vibe.

13 · Tamper-evident sticker

Striped "VOID IF REMOVED" sticker peels off with a skewed transition. Reads as a single-use protective seal.

14 · Pull-tab ring

Soda-can ring on top — click the ring to "pull" and the cover lifts upward. Tactile and unambiguous.

15 · Hold-to-confirm ring

Press AND hold. A conic-gradient ring fills over ~1.2 s, then flips to green = confirmed. No flip animation — pure state communication.

16 · Fingerprint scanner

Skeuomorphic biometric pad. Hold to "scan" — red scan line animates while held. Premium-feeling.

17 · Guarded toggle switch

A small bar physically blocks the toggle. Click the bar to lift it, then drag the toggle to fire. Two-step interaction.

18 · Triple-arm sequence

Three small "ARM" buttons must be pressed in left-to-right order. Then the red mini-button enables. Highest deliberation cost; for true nuclear actions.

19 · Camera iris / aperture

8-blade iris opens with a satisfying rotate. Reveals the button at the centre. Unusual + photogenic.

20 · Defusal — clip the red wire

Four colour-coded wires. Only the red one fires. Other clicks shake the screen. For developers + people who watch action movies.
