Overview

Aseptic technique is the set of practices used to prevent contamination by microorganisms during biomanufacturing. In sterile production environments, even brief lapses in technique can compromise a batch and the patients who depend on it.

Why it matters

  • Patient safety — contaminated product can cause infection or worse.
  • Regulatory compliance — FDA 21 CFR Part 211, EU GMP Annex 1.
  • Cost — a single failed batch can run into millions of dollars.

Learning objectives
