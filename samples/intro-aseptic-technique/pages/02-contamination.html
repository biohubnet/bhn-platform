Contamination Sources

Most contamination events in aseptic processing trace back to one of four sources, often summarized as the four P's: People, Procedures, Premises, Plant.

People

Operators are the largest source. Skin shedding, exhaled aerosols, and improper gowning introduce particulates and viable organisms into the cleanroom.

Procedures

Workflow design — line clearance, transfer technique, sampling — directly governs how often the product is exposed to non-sterile surfaces or air.

Premises

HVAC, room classification (ISO 5 / Grade A through D), pressure cascade, surface finish. Failures here are systemic.

Plant

Equipment cleanability, single-use vs. cleaned-in-place, sterilization validation, and change-part hygiene.

Quick rule of thumb: when something fails an environmental monitoring check, walk back through the four P's in order — most root causes show up at the first one.
