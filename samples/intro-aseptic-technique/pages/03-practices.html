Best Practices

The core aseptic practices below apply to nearly every operation inside an ISO 5 / Grade A zone. They are deliberately conservative — when in doubt, default to the stricter behavior.

Gowning

  • Never touch the outside of sterile garments with bare or gloved skin.
  • Sit only on the gowning bench during the donning sequence.
  • Change gloves any time they touch a non-sterile surface.

Movement

  • Move slowly. Rapid motion creates turbulent air and dislodges particulates from your gown.
  • Never pass your hands or body over an open product container — work from the side.
  • Avoid talking inside the critical zone whenever possible.

Transfer

  • Disinfect every surface that crosses the Grade A/B boundary, then dwell for the validated time.
  • Use sterile, single-use materials wherever they exist.
  • Document the transfer in the batch record before the next operation begins.
